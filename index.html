How long does setup take?

Most schools are ready within a week. Importing students from a spreadsheet takes minutes, and our team helps with the rest.

Do teachers and parents need their own accounts?

Teachers get a login to mark attendance and see timetables. Parents receive announcements and reminders by message and email.

Can we manage more than one campus?

Yes. The Campus plan supports several campuses with separate classes and shared reports.

Does it support right-to-left languages?

Yes. The interface mirrors for right-to-left languages and offers light and dark themes.

Is our data safe?

Student and fee records sit in one access-controlled system. Each staff member only sees what their role allows.
