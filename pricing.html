FAQ

Pricing questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Start with the plan that fits today and move to a larger one when your school needs more.

Is there a way to try it first?

Request a demo and we will walk you through the dashboard before you decide.

How do I get a Campus quote?

Contact us with a few details about your school and we will prepare a quote for you.

What do the prices include?

Prices on this page are per school per month and exclude applicable taxes. Contact us for multi-campus or trust pricing.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, net banking, cards and bank transfer. Yearly plans can be invoiced.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Onboarding and data import are included with every plan.

What happens if we go over our student limit?

We will contact you to move to the next plan. Nothing is switched off without notice.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us about your school and we will recommend the right one.
